About XERF

XERF is a monopolar radiofrequency (RF) lifting device from Cynosure Lutronic. Like Thermage it is RF-based, but it uses two frequencies — 2MHz and 6.78MHz — to deliver energy at different depths. Your doctor plans the 400 shots after checking your skin on the day.
